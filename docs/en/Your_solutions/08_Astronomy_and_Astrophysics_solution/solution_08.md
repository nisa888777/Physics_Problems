# 8. Interplanetary Travel Times to Mars

**Problem Statement:** How much time would it take to get from Earth to Mars when Mars is at its closest approach ($55 \, \text{million km}$)? We will evaluate this duration across three distinct engineering cases:

---

## **Case a) Communication via Speed of Light**

We evaluate a radio or laser signal traveling through the vacuum of space at the constant speed of light ($v_c \approx 300,000 \, \text{km/s}$):

* **Primary Equation:**
$$t = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

* **Mathematical Substitution:**
$$t = \frac{55,000,000 \, \text{km}}{300,000 \, \text{km/s}} \approx 183.33 \, \text{seconds}$$

* **Unit Conversion (To Minutes):**
$$t = \frac{183.33}{60} \approx 3.06 \, \text{minutes}$$

---

## **Case b) Travel via Modern Spacecraft**

We evaluate a deep-space probe cruising through orbit at a constant technological speed of $v = 40,000 \, \text{km/h}$:

* **Primary Equation:**
$$t = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

* **Mathematical Substitution:**
$$t = \frac{55,000,000 \, \text{km}}{40,000 \, \text{km/h}} = 1375 \, \text{hours}$$

* **Unit Conversion (To Days):**
$$t = \frac{1375}{24} \approx 57.29 \, \text{days}$$

---

## **Case c) Travel via Commercial Airplane**

We imagine a theoretical commercial jet flying continuously through space at a typical cruise speed of $v = 900 \, \text{km/h}$:

* **Primary Equation:**
$$t = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$

* **Mathematical Substitution:**
$$t = \frac{55,000,000 \, \text{km}}{900 \, \text{km/h}} \approx 61111.11 \, \text{hours}$$

* **Unit Conversion (To Days):**
$$\text{Total Days} = \frac{61111.11}{24} \approx 2546.3 \, \text{days}$$

* **Unit Conversion (To Calendar Years):**
$$t = \frac{2546.3}{365} \approx 6.98 \, \text{years}$$

---

## **Summary of Final Results**

The computed travel times beautifully highlight the massive spatial scale of our solar system:

* **Light-speed Message:** $\approx \mathbf{3.06 \, \text{minutes}}$
* **Spacecraft Rocket:** $\approx \mathbf{57.3 \, \text{days}}$
* **Commercial Jet:** $\approx \mathbf{6.98 \, \text{years}}$
