# 5. Percentage Calculation of Uncertainty

**Problem Statement:** A measurement of time is experimentally recorded as $t = 5.45 \pm 0.22 \, \text{seconds}$. Our objective is to evaluate the precision of this measurement by calculating its **percentage uncertainty**.

---

### **I. Theoretical Definition**
Percentage uncertainty represents the ratio of the absolute uncertainty ($\Delta t$) to the measured value ($t$), scaled to a percentage. It acts as a direct indicator of experimental precision.

* **Fundamental Governing Formula:**
$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{\Delta t}{t} \right) \times 100\%$$

---

### **II. Step-by-Step Numerical Evaluation**
To find the final precision value, we process the extracted variables through our formula systematically:

1. **Identify Extracted Variables:**
   * Measured time ($t$) = $5.45 \, \text{seconds}$
   * Absolute uncertainty ($\Delta t$) = $0.22 \, \text{seconds}$

2. **Apply Variables to the Formula:**
$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = \left( \frac{0.22}{5.45} \right) \times 100\%$$

3. **Perform Algebraic Division:**
$$\frac{0.22}{5.45} \approx 0.040366$$

4. **Multiply by One Hundred for Final Percentage:**
$$\text{Percentage Uncertainty} = 0.040366 \times 100\% \approx 4.04\%$$

---

### **Conclusion**
The percentage uncertainty of the recorded time measurement is approximately **$4.04\%$**. This relatively small percentage indicates that the time measurement has a high level of experimental precision and a very low error rate.
