## 2. Range Optimization
**Problem:** Show analytically that the maximum range $R(\theta) = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$ is achieved at a launch angle of $45^\circ$.



* **Step 1: The Range Formula**
  The analytical expression for range is: $R(\theta) = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$

  

* **Step 2: Analytical Optimization**
  To find the maximum range, we examine the term $\sin(2\theta)$. Since the maximum value of the sine function is 1:
  $\sin(2\theta) = 1$

  

* **Step 3: Solving for the Angle**
  $\sin(2\theta) = 1 \Rightarrow 2\theta = 90^\circ$
  $\theta = 45^\circ$

  

* **Conclusion:** Therefore, the maximum range for a given initial velocity is always achieved at a launch angle of $45^\circ$.
