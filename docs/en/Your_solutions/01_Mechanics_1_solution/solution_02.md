## 2. Range Optimization
**Problem:** Prove that the maximum range is achieved at a launch angle of $45^\circ$.

* **Step 1: The Range Formula**
  The analytical expression for range is: $R(\theta) = \frac{v_0^2 \sin(2\theta)}{g}$
* **Step 2: Maximizing the Function**
  To find the maximum of $R$, we look at the term $\sin(2\theta)$. The maximum value any sine function can take is **1**.
* **Step 3: Solving for the Angle**
  $\sin(2\theta) = 1 \Rightarrow 2\theta = 90^\circ$
  $\theta = 45^\circ$
* **Conclusion:** For any given initial velocity, launching at $45^\circ$ provides the maximum possible distance.
