## 1. Projectile Motion
**Problem:** A projectile is fired from the ground with $v_0 = 100 \text{ m/s}$ at an angle of $37^\circ$ above the horizontal. Assume no air resistance.



* **Step 1: Calculate Velocity Components**
  - $v_{0x} = v_0 \cdot \cos(37^\circ) = 100 \cdot 0.8 = 80 \text{ m/s}$
  - $v_{0y} = v_0 \cdot \sin(37^\circ) = 100 \cdot 0.6 = 60 \text{ m/s}$
 
  - 

* **Step 2: Determine the Time of Flight ($T$)**
  The total time in the air is calculated by:
  $T = \frac{2 \cdot v_{0y}}{g} = \frac{2 \cdot 60}{9.8} \approx 12.24 \text{ s}$

  

* **Step 3: Determine the Maximum Height ($H$)**
  The highest point reached is:
  $H = \frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g} = \frac{60^2}{19.6} \approx 183.67 \text{ m}$

  

* **Step 4: Determine the Range ($R$)**
  The total horizontal distance covered:
  $R = v_{0x} \cdot T = 80 \cdot 12.24 \approx 979.2 \text{ m}$
