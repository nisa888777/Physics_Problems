## 1. Projectile Motion
**Question:** A projectile is fired from the ground with $v_0 = 100 \text{ m/s}$ at $\theta = 37^\circ$. Determine the time of flight, maximum height, and range.

* **Step 1 (Velocity Components):**
  $v_{0x} = 100 \cdot \cos(37^\circ) = 80 \text{ m/s}$
  $v_{0y} = 100 \cdot \sin(37^\circ) = 60 \text{ m/s}$
* **Step 2 (Time of Flight):**
  $T = \frac{2v_{0y}}{g} = \frac{2 \cdot 60}{9.8} \approx 12.24 \text{ s}$
* **Step 3 (Maximum Height):**
  $H = \frac{v_{0y}^2}{2g} = \frac{60^2}{19.6} \approx 183.67 \text{ m}$
* **Step 4 (Range):**
  $R = v_{0x} \cdot T = 80 \cdot 12.24 \approx 979.2 \text{ m}$
