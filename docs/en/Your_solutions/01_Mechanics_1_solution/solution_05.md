## 5. Relative Velocity
**Problem:** A boat crossing a $200\text{m}$ wide river flowing at $2 \text{ m/s}$.

* **Step 1: Determining Heading Angle**
  To travel straight across (North), the boat's horizontal velocity must cancel the river's speed ($2 \text{ m/s}$ East):
  $v_{boat} \cdot \sin(\theta) = v_{river} \Rightarrow 5 \cdot \sin(\theta) = 2$
  $\sin(\theta) = 0.4 \Rightarrow \theta \approx 23.58^\circ$ (Heading West of North)
* **Step 2: Calculating Effective Speed**
  The speed directed across the river is the vertical component:
  $v_y = \sqrt{v_{boat}^2 - v_{river}^2} = \sqrt{25 - 4} = \sqrt{21} \approx 4.58 \text{ m/s}$
* **Step 3: Calculating Time**
  $t = \frac{\text{Distance}}{v_y} = \frac{200}{4.58} \approx 43.67 \text{ s}$
