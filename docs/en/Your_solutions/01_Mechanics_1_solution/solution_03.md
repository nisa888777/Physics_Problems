## 3. Path Intersection
**Problem:** Alice moves along $A(t) = (2+t, 8-3t)$ and Bob moves along $B(t) = (2t-1, 2t+2)$. Determine if their paths intersect.



* **Step 1: Equate X-coordinates to find time ($t$)**
  $x_A(t) = x_B(t) \Rightarrow 2 + t = 2t - 1$
  $t = 3 \text{ seconds}$

  

* **Step 2: Check Y-coordinates at $t=3$**
  - For Alice: $y_A(3) = 8 - 3(3) = 8 - 9 = -1$
  - For Bob: $y_B(3) = 2(3) + 2 = 6 + 2 = 8$
 
  - 

* **Conclusion:** Since $y_A(3) \neq y_B(3)$, the paths do not intersect at the same time. While they have the same horizontal position at $t=3$, they are at different heights.
