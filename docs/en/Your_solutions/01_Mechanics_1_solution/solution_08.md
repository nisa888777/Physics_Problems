## 8. Circular Motion

**Problem:** Calculate the centripetal acceleration at Earth's equator ($R = 6378 \text{ km}$).

* **Step 1: Velocity** $v = \frac{2\pi R}{T} = \frac{2 \cdot \pi \cdot 6,378,000}{86,400} \approx 463.8 \text{ m/s}$
* **Step 2: Centripetal Acceleration** $a_c = \frac{v^2}{R} = \frac{463.8^2}{6,378,000} \approx 0.0337 \text{ m/s}^2$
