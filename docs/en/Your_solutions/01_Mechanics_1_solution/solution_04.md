## 4. Vector Calculus
**Problem:** The position of an object is given by $\vec{r}(t) = (3t^2)\hat{i} + (5t - 8t^2)\hat{j}$. Find velocity and acceleration vectors.




* **Step 1: Find Velocity Vector $\vec{v}(t)$**
  Velocity is the first derivative of position with respect to time:
  $\vec{v}(t) = \frac{d\vec{r}}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}(3t^2)\hat{i} + \frac{d}{dt}(5t - 8t^2)\hat{j}$
  $\vec{v}(t) = (6t)\hat{i} + (5 - 16t)\hat{j}$


  

* **Step 2: Find Acceleration Vector $\vec{a}(t)$**
  Acceleration is the derivative of velocity:
  $\vec{a}(t) = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}(6t)\hat{i} + \frac{d}{dt}(5 - 16t)\hat{j}$
  $\vec{a}(t) = (6)\hat{i} + (-16)\hat{j}$
