## 4. Vector Calculus
**Problem:** Find velocity and acceleration from position vector $\vec{r}(t) = (3t^2)\hat{i} + (5t - 8t^2)\hat{j}$.

* **Step 1: Velocity Vector ($\vec{v}$)**
  Velocity is the first derivative of position:
  $\vec{v}(t) = \frac{d}{dt}(3t^2)\hat{i} + \frac{d}{dt}(5t - 8t^2)\hat{j}$
  $\vec{v}(t) = (6t)\hat{i} + (5 - 16t)\hat{j}$
* **Step 2: Acceleration Vector ($\vec{a}$)**
  Acceleration is the derivative of velocity:
  $\vec{a}(t) = \frac{d}{dt}(6t)\hat{i} + \frac{d}{dt}(5 - 16t)\hat{j}$
  $\vec{a}(t) = (6)\hat{i} + (-16)\hat{j}$
* **Result:** The acceleration is constant at $6$ in the x-direction and $-16$ in the y-direction.
