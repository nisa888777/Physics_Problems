## 9. Momentum Comparison
**Problem:** Which has greater momentum: a 2-gram fly flying at $10 \text{ m/s}$ or a 60-gram tennis ball moving at $1 \text{ m/s}$?

* **Step 1: Convert Units to SI (kg)**
  - Fly mass ($m_1$): $2 \text{ g} = 0.002 \text{ kg}$
  - Tennis ball mass ($m_2$): $60 \text{ g} = 0.060 \text{ kg}$
  - 

* **Step 2: Calculate Momentum ($p = m \cdot v$)**
  - **Fly:** $p_1 = 0.002 \text{ kg} \cdot 10 \text{ m/s} = 0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$
  - **Tennis Ball:** $p_2 = 0.060 \text{ kg} \cdot 1 \text{ m/s} = 0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$

* **Step 3: Comparison**
  $0.06 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s} > 0.02 \text{ kg}\cdot\text{m/s}$

* **Conclusion:** The **tennis ball** has greater momentum than the fly.
